German Power Price — Forecast & Risk

DE-LU day-ahead, hourly. A quantile forecast, and what it implies for a position held in the market.

This tool predicts the hourly price of electricity in Germany, one day ahead, as a full range of plausible outcomes rather than a single number — and translates that range into the potential loss on a position of a given size.

New here? A 60-second guide to the numbers below

Why isn't there just one number? Power prices can move a lot, sometimes more than expected — think of a cold, windless week when renewable output drops and demand rises. So instead of one guess, the model gives a range of plausible prices for each hour, along with how confident it is.

What the fan chart shows: the shaded area is that range — how wide it is tells you how uncertain the model currently is. The blue line is its single best guess (the "median"). The black line is what the price actually turned out to be, so you can see how the forecast performed with hindsight.

What "potential loss" means: if you held a position of a given size (adjustable below), how much money could you plausibly lose in a single day? That's calculated at a 99% confidence level — a loss beyond that number should only happen about 1 day in 100.

Tip: drag a box on the chart to zoom into any period — the numbers above update to reflect exactly what's visible. Double-click the chart to reset.
